Setting up

Rules of the land

1

Plant up to 160 acres, in 10-acre units.

2

Plant no more than three different crops in one year.

3

Buy as many animals as you can afford. They don’t use any of your acres.

4

Seed, labor, supplies, taxes, and upkeep are already in the price.

5

Money you don’t spend goes on the Savings line.

Rule 4 means the price per acre or per animal is the only cost you pay for that crop or herd. Your fixed costs are separate.
