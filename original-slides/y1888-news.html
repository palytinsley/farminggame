The Platte Floods the Valley

Latest harvest news from along the river

❖ Heavy mountain snow melted, and the Platte River spilled over the farmland.

❖ Field peas, planted late, came through best.

❖ Cattle drowned trying to reach feed.

❖ Sheep prices are up. Hog prices dropped.

1888

The Platte gets its water from Rocky Mountain snow, so a heavy winter in the mountains can mean a spring flood on the plains.
