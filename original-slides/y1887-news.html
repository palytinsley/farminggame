Central Nebraska  ·  Harvest Edition, 1887

Grasshoppers Strip the Fields

Latest harvest news from along the river

❖ A hot, dry spring brought a plague of grasshoppers. Most crops are badly damaged.

❖ Range herds still haven’t recovered from the killing winter, so cattle prices are very good.

❖ Sheep prices are down.

❖ Hog prices are very good.

1887

History note: the worst Rocky Mountain locust swarms hit Nebraska in the mid-1870s (1874–1877), not 1887. The species was extinct by about 1902, and nobody is entirely sure why.
